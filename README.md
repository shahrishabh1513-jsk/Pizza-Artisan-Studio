## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🎨 **Interactive Pizza Builder** | Click on ingredients to add them to your pizza in real-time |
| 🌀 **Stunning Animations** | Toppings appear with bounce effects, floating pizza, particle effects |
| 🧀 **9+ Delicious Ingredients** | Sauce, Cheese, Pepperoni, Olives, Mushroom, Bell Pepper, Onion, Basil, Tomato |
| 📊 **Points System** | Base (5 pts) + each topping (2 pts) = Total pizza score |
| 🧾 **Beautiful Recipe Bill** | Review your creation with star ratings and fun facts |
| 📱 **Fully Responsive** | Works perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop |
| 🎭 **Loading Animation** | Adorable chef and spinning pizza loader |
| 🖨️ **Print Recipe** | Print your pizza bill as a recipe card |
| ✨ **Particle Effects** | Floating particles when adding ingredients |
| 🎯 **Reset Option** | Start fresh anytime with reset button |

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Start with Base** - Every pizza starts with a fresh dough base (5 pts)
2. **Add Ingredients** - Click on any ingredient card to add it to your pizza
3. **Watch it Come Alive** - See toppings appear with beautiful animations
4. **Build Your Creation** - Mix and match up to 9 different toppings
5. **Review Your Pizza** - Click "Review Pizza" to see your recipe bill
6. **Print & Share** - Print your pizza recipe or create a new one

**Scoring System:**
- Pizza Base: ⭐ 5 points
- Each Topping: ⭐ +2 points
- Total Score = Base + (Number of Toppings × 2)
